# Client checklist — what we need from a new restaurant

This is a **client-facing** document. It is written for a restaurant owner, not for
a developer. Use it during the pitch and to collect everything needed to build and
launch their site. It is safe to copy the questions or the ready-to-send WhatsApp
message at the bottom straight to the owner.

Do not add developer jargon, internal architecture, or credentials to this file.

## What the restaurant gets

- A fast, mobile-friendly website that shows your full menu and prices.
- Customers browse the menu and send a **table reservation request on WhatsApp** —
  no apps, no payment gateway, no commission.
- A photo gallery of your food and your space.
- The owner can update **prices, sold-out items and today's specials** themselves,
  for free, from a simple Google Sheet.
- Website hosting costs the restaurant **nothing per month**.
- When live, the site can appear on Google and be shared on Facebook and WhatsApp
  with a proper preview.
- Links to your delivery apps (PickMe, Uber Eats, Foodpanda) can be added.
- Sinhala and Tamil can be added on request (English is included).

## 1. Business basics

- [ ] Restaurant / brand name (exactly as it should appear).
- [ ] A short tagline or one-line description.
- [ ] A short "our story" paragraph (a few sentences; we can polish it).
- [ ] Logo file, if they have one (PNG or SVG on a plain or transparent background).
- [ ] Brand colours — or tell us to choose for them.
- [ ] Any font or style they must match (optional).
- [ ] Photos for the top of the home page and the gallery.

## 2. Contact details

- [ ] **WhatsApp number that should receive reservation requests** (with country
      code).
- [ ] Phone number shown on the site.
- [ ] Email (optional — for display only; requests go to WhatsApp).
- [ ] Facebook / Instagram / TikTok links (optional).
- [ ] Who replies to reservation requests, and roughly when they are available.

## 3. Location and opening hours

- [ ] Full address as it should be written.
- [ ] Google Maps location (a shared pin/link is perfect).
- [ ] Opening hours for each day, including closed days and lunch/dinner split.
- [ ] Special or holiday hours, if any (optional).
- [ ] Parking / landmark directions, if useful (optional).

## 4. How they take reservations

- [ ] Do you take walk-ins, or is booking recommended/preferred?
- [ ] Largest party you can seat or wish to accept through the site (the site takes
      requests for up to 8; larger groups are arranged on WhatsApp).
- [ ] Preferred reservation time slots you would like customers to choose from
      (e.g. 12:00–14:00, 19:00–22:00).
- [ ] How much notice you usually need (e.g. "same day is fine", "book a day ahead").
- [ ] Do any bookings need a deposit or a phone call? (We will show a note; the owner
      confirms details in chat — no payments on the site.)
- [ ] Occasions you would like customers to be able to mention (birthday,
      anniversary, business dinner, celebration).

## 5. Menu and prices

For every dish, we need:

- [ ] Category (e.g. Starters, Rice & Curry, Kottu, From the Grill, Vegetarian,
      Desserts, Drinks).
- [ ] Dish name.
- [ ] Price in LKR.
- [ ] A short one-sentence description (optional but recommended).
- [ ] Vegetarian or not (a small marker is shown for veg dishes).
- [ ] Spice level, if useful (mild / medium / hot).
- [ ] A photo (optional to start — we can launch with placeholders and add photos
      later).
- [ ] Sinhala and/or Tamil name, if they have it (optional; we can arrange
      translation).
- [ ] Which dishes are your specialities or should be featured on the home page.
- [ ] Any dishes that are often sold out or only available on certain days.

A simple table is enough — the owner can fill it in a note or a spreadsheet:

| Category     | Dish                 | Price (LKR) | Veg | Spice | Description                                  | Photo? |
| ------------ | -------------------- | ----------- | --- | ----- | -------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| Rice & Curry | Chicken Rice & Curry | 1450        | no  | 2     | Rice with chicken curry and five side dishes | yes    |
| Kottu        | Chicken Cheese Kottu | 1650        | no  | 2     | Chopped roti with chicken, egg and cheese    | no     |

We will handle dish IDs, image sizing and formatting — the owner just supplies the
above.

## 6. Photos

- [ ] A wide photo for the top of the home page (your best-looking dish or table).
- [ ] Gallery photos: signature dishes, the restaurant interior, the kitchen, the
      street front (as many as possible — natural light, no screenshots or
      watermarks).
- [ ] The owner or team (optional).
- [ ] Any logos or signage.

Tips to share with the owner:

- Send the **original** photos, not forwards/screenshots. WhatsApp compresses
  images — for important photos, send as a **document** or share a Google
  Drive / Google Photos link.
- Natural daylight or warm evening light both work; keep the background clean.
- It is fine to start with a few photos and add more later.

## 7. Price and availability updates (Google Sheet)

- [ ] A Gmail address the restaurant controls (ideally a dedicated restaurant
      account, not a personal one) so we can set up their menu sheet.
- [ ] The person who will update prices and mark dishes sold out.
- [ ] We set up the Sheet, show them how to use it, and keep a backup of the current
      prices on our side.

## 8. Delivery apps and ordering

- [ ] Links to your PickMe Food / Uber Eats / Foodpanda pages, if you have them (or
      tell us to leave this out).
- [ ] Whether you want customers to order on WhatsApp instead (we can point order
      buttons at your WhatsApp).

## 9. Domain and languages

- [ ] Preferred web address. A free option is `<restaurant-name>.zuvo.lk`. The
      owner can also use their own domain if they have one (or want to buy one).
- [ ] Languages needed: English is included. Sinhala and/or Tamil can be added.
- [ ] Which language should be the default?

## 10. Sign-off

- [ ] The person who approves the final site.
- [ ] Any existing brand guidelines or marketing material to match.
- [ ] A deadline or event the site should be ready for (optional).

## What we do NOT need

- No payment gateway or online payments.
- No booking/calendar account.
- No bank details or payment credentials.
- No hosting or server accounts — hosting is included and free.
- No technical skills from the owner.
- No professional writing or professional photos to get started.

## What happens next

1. You send us the items above (in any order).
2. We build the site and show you a preview link.
3. You review and tell us what to change.
4. We connect the restaurant's WhatsApp number and Google Sheet.
5. The site goes live on the chosen web address.
6. We show the owner how to update prices, mark dishes sold out, and flag specials.

```text

9. A Gmail address you control so I can set up a simple menu sheet you can update yourself.

Send whatever you have first, and we can fill in the rest later.
```
