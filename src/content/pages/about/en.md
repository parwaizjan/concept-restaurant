---
title: Our story
---

Spice & Ember is a modern Sri Lankan kitchen on Galle Face Terrace. We opened with
one idea: the food we grew up with, cooked properly and served in a room you want
to linger in.

Our kitchen works over fire and in clay. Curries are pounded fresh each morning,
roti is chopped to order, and the seafood comes in from the coast. Nothing sits
under a lamp — if it is on the menu, it is made when you ask for it.

Whether it is a quick rice and curry at lunch, kottu after work, or a long dinner
with friends, the table is yours. We keep our prices clear, our spice honest, and
you can request a table in a minute on WhatsApp.
